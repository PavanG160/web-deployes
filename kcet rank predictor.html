📚 KCET Counselling Guide

1. The Counselling Process

  • Document Verification: You must visit a nodal center with original documents. Once verified, you get a Secret Key.
  • Registration: Use the Secret Key to create a password on the KEA portal.
  • Mock Allotment: KEA runs a "trial" result to show what college you might get. You can change your options after this.

2. How to do Option Entry?

This is where you list your college preferences. Priority matters! KEA checks your #1 choice first. If available, you get it. If not, it moves to #2.

  • List as many colleges as possible (no limit).
  • Put your "Dream Colleges" at the top, even if your rank is low.
  • Put "Backup Colleges" at the bottom.

3. Mistakes to Avoid ⚠️

  • Entering too few options: You might end up with NO seat.
  • Sharing Secret Key: Never share your key or password with anyone.
  • Forgetting to Logout: Always click 'Logout' after saving options to prevent data loss.
  • Ignoring Mock Results: Use the mock results to rearrange your list realistically.
